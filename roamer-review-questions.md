# "Roamer's Review" — Candidate Questions

A traveler's take on a city after visiting. Pick the 10 best.
Assumed same setup as the local interview: signed-in members only, reviewed before publishing.
Auto-filled: name (from account), city. Optional fields: "When did you visit?" and "How long did you stay?"

## Expectations vs. reality
1. What did you expect before arriving — and what actually surprised you?
2. What did the guidebooks/Instagram get wrong about this city?
3. What's one thing that was even better than the hype?
4. What disappointed you, and would it stop you from recommending the city?

## The experience
5. Describe the city in three words now that you've been.
6. What's the moment from your trip you still think about?
7. What was your favorite "accidental" discovery — something you didn't plan?
8. What did an average day of your trip actually look like?
9. Did you feel welcome? How were the locals with travelers?
10. How easy was it to meet people (locals or other travelers)?

## Practical takeaways
11. What do you wish you'd known before arriving?
12. What did you overpack or overplan — and what did you wish you'd brought or booked?
13. How far did your money go? Cheaper or pricier than you expected, and where did costs sneak up?
14. How did you get around, and what would you do differently?
15. How many days does this city really need?
16. Where did you stay, and would you pick that neighborhood again?
17. How safe did you feel, including at night or as a solo traveler?

## Food & highlights
18. Best thing you ate — and where?
19. One place, activity, or experience you'd tell every visitor not to skip?
20. What's popular that you'd honestly tell people to skip?

## The verdict
21. Who is this city perfect for — and who should probably pick somewhere else?
22. Would you go back? What would you do differently the second time?
23. What's the one tip you'd give someone landing there tomorrow?
24. If you could relive one hour of your trip, which hour would it be?
