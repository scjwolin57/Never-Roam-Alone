Community Contributions

Never Roam Alone is built by roamers, for roamers. Three ways to help fellow travelers: write for the blog, share what a city was like to visit, or tell travelers about the city you live in.

1 · Write for the blog

Share a Blog Post

Been somewhere memorable? Pitch a story from the road. Our editors read every pitch and reply by email, and a published post counts toward your Contribution score.

2 · You've visited

Become a Trusted Traveler

Share a Traveler's Take on a city you've been to. We check every interview before it goes on the guide, and each one adds to your Contribution score. At 100 points you get the Trusted Traveler badge, and the thumbs up other travelers give your contributions build your Trusted Traveler Rating.

3 · You live there

Share a Local's Perspective

Live in one of our cities? Tell travelers what it's really like, in your own words. We use the home city on your profile to take you to the right guide.
