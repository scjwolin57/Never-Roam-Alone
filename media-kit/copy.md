## What's on the site

- **Interactive world map** (home) — every city as a bubble sized by annual international visitors; click any bubble to open its guide.
- **City Guides** (cities.html) — browse and search all 893 guides; sort A–Z or by popularity.
- **Destination Finder** (choose.html) — answer a few questions and get cities matched to your budget, travel time, season and visa rules.
- **Compare Cities** (compare.html) — two cities side by side: costs, weather, safety, walkability, popularity and visa rules.
- **Where to Go This Month** (best-time-to-visit.html) — cities with ideal weather and festivals worth planning around, month by month.
- **Top Visited** and **Underrated Picks** — the most-visited cities on the site, and the ones travelers overlook.
- **Ask A Roamer** (askaroamer.html) — real questions answered by travelers who have been there.
- **Blog** — stories, tips and travel wisdom from the community.
- **Community & Trusted Travelers** (community.html) — pitch a blog post, or apply to become a Trusted Traveler who answers questions and vouches for places.
- **Trip planner, favorites and profiles** — save cities, build a day-by-day itinerary, and connect with other roamers in town.
- **Five languages** — English, Español, Français, Italiano, 中文.

## Inside every city guide

Season & how long to stay · Fast facts (temperature and rain by season, population, visitors, walk score, safety index, solo comfort, women-traveling-solo rating) · Emergency numbers & embassy finder · Languages, religion & common phrases · Connectivity (Wi-Fi speeds, free public Wi-Fi, tourist SIM prices, operators, eSIM comparison) · Electricity & plug types · Currency, card acceptance & live converter · Typical costs (hotel, meal, taxi, drinks) · Getting to and from the airport · Getting around · Neighborhood guide with lodging tiers · Top sights · Day trips · Events · Traveler's Take & Local's Perspective · Stories · Roamers in town
