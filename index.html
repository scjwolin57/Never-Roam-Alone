About Me

Hi, I'm Jeff

Founder & chief roamer · 60+ countries

I got bit by the travel bug a bit later than many, but like they say, it's never too late to start anything. What started for me as an escape from the stresses of life has turned into a love of exploration and discovery. I have been fortunate enough to have visited over 60 countries in 5 continents so far, with plans for many more.

I started this website to share my experiences and to hopefully provide useful tools for fellow travelers to successfully navigate their next adventures. I hope that you find them helpful.

— happy roaming
