About Me

Hi, I'm Jeff

Founder & chief roamer · 60+ countries

I got bit by the travel bug a bit later than most, but as they say, it's never too late to start anything. What started for me as an escape from the stresses of life has turned into a love of exploration and discovery. I have been fortunate enough to have visited over 60 countries, spanning 5 continents so far, with plans for many more.

I started this website to share my experiences and to hopefully provide useful information for fellow travelers to successfully book and/or navigate their next adventures. I hope that you find the website helpful. If you have any questions or comments regarding anything on the site, please reach out to me with the feedback link at the bottom of the page.

— happy roaming
