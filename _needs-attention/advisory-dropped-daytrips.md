# Day trips dropped because of a government travel advisory (listed 2026-09-23)

**New rule (Jeff, 2026-09-23, decisions.md):** one country's travel advice never disqualifies research. The site
is for travellers of every nationality and shows the US, UK and Canadian advisories on the city page itself.

The day-trip research briefs had a rule nobody approved: "reject anything in a US Level 4 or UK FCDO
advise-against-all-travel area". It is removed from the briefs now. The places below were dropped or never
added **mainly because of that rule**. Nothing here has been put back yet.

Places dropped for other reasons (too far, border shut, flight-only, a guide city) are not on this list, even
where the notes also mention an advisory: Kuwait City's Iraqi and Iranian towns, Dushanbe's Kunduz and
Mazar-e Sharif, Yangon's Myawadi, Vilnius's Baranovichi, Barysaw and Minsk, Asmara's Kassala, and Tel Aviv's Gaza.
