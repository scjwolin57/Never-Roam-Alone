# Flickr permission request: message to paste

*For the rows in `flickr-permission-shortlist.csv` with a candidate photo. Open the photo, click the photographer's
name, then "Message" (Flickr calls it "Flickrmail"). Fill in the three bracketed parts. When you send one, set that
row's `status` to "asked" and the date in `notes`; when they answer, "yes" or "no". Rows marked "free-licence"
need no message: the licence already allows use with credit.*

---

**Subject:** Permission to use your photo of [LANDMARK] on a free travel guide

Hi [NAME],

I run Never Roam Alone (neverroamalone.com), a free city guide site for travelers. Your photo of [LANDMARK] in
[CITY] is the best one I've found of it, and I'd love to use it on our [CITY] guide:

[PHOTO LINK]

It would appear as the picture for that landmark, with your name credited under it and linked to your Flickr page.
We'd show it at web size only, never sell it, and take it down any time you ask.

Would that be all right with you? Either way, thank you for sharing such a great shot.

Jeff
Never Roam Alone

---

## Notes

- Keep it short and personal; one landmark per message works best.
- "Yes" by Flickrmail is the written permission we keep: copy the reply into the row's `notes` (date + their words).
- If they ask for a specific credit wording or link, put it in `notes` so the credit matches.
