# Plug types for the 35 territory and state guides: research log (2026-10-15)

Jeff, "research the 35 open plug types". Four research agents, one per region (`_guidebuild/plugs/BRIEF.md`, outputs `out_1..4.json`), each told to look first for the place's own source (utility, government or tourism board), then reputable travel guidance, and to compare worldstandards.eu with Wikipedia's "Mains electricity by country". The IEC World Plugs page refused access (403) and the CIA World Factbook pages are retired, so no source here is the IEC's own.

**Result:** 33 guides now have `power` (sheet and citydata together). 2 stay empty (Åland, Svalbard: no source names their plug letters). 3 have plug letters and frequency but no voltage because sources conflict (Aruba 120V or 127V, Bermuda 110V or 120V, Saint Helena 230V or 240V). Confidence: `confirmed` = the place's own source, or two independent sources agree on every field; `likely` = one decent source and nothing contradicting. Nothing was copied from a sovereign parent without a source naming the place.

Where a reference site listed an extra letter that nothing else supported (type C beside G for Gibraltar, E for the Faroe Islands and Greenland, D for the French departments), it was left out. Wikipedia's "230/400 V" style figures are single-phase plus three-phase pairs; the single-phase figure is used.
