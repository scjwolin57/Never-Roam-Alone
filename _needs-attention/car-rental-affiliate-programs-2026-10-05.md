# Car rental affiliate programs: DiscoverCars first, Rentalcars.com second

*Research date 2026-10-05. Jeff asked for programs with wide worldwide coverage, high conversion, and many rental companies on one search. Only brokers and comparison sites are listed; single-brand programs (Hertz, Avis, Enterprise, Sixt) fail "multiple companies" and are left out. "P" = read on the program's own page; "S" = secondary (network listing, directory, blog). No network dashboard was logged into, so rates should be re-checked at application time.*

## The answer

1. **DiscoverCars** is the best fit on all three tests. 1,000+ suppliers, 145+ countries (S), 70% of its profit on the rental plus 30% of its Full Coverage sales, about US$20 a booking, a 365-day cookie, and the only program that publishes a conversion rate: **3 to 5.5%** (all P, discovercars.com/affiliate). Joinable direct, or on CJ, which is also the Booking.com hotel route.
2. **Rentalcars.com** (Booking Holdings) has the widest stated reach: 800+ companies, 60,000 locations, 160 countries, 40+ languages. 6% on "pay now" bookings, only 1% on "pay later", and an in-session window only, no cookie (P, Awin advertiser spotlight, June 2023). Strong brand trust lifts conversion, but no figure is published. Via CJ for Europe and the Middle East, Awin for the Americas and Asia-Pacific (S).
3. **VIP Cars** is a solid backup: 800+ suppliers, 160+ countries, 22,000+ locations, "up to 75%" of its margin, deep links and plain links (P, affiliate.vipcars.com). EUR 200 payout floor, PayPal or bank (P, terms). Cookie not published.
4. **Expedia cars** at 1.5% (P, commission terms, read for the hotel note 2026-09-24) comes free with the Expedia account Jeff is already applying for. Low rate, no extra paperwork.

Conversion: no program other than DiscoverCars publishes a rate. The one third-party figure found, Webgains for EconomyBookings, reads **0.05%**, which is very low. Expect real conversion to depend on where the link sits (airport block and day-trip cards beat a generic banner).

## Things to know before applying

- **Travelpayouts is off the table** (decisions.md 2026-09-18). Several programs push it as their main route (QEEQ, Localrent, DiscoverCars' "Car Hire" variant). Use direct or CJ/Awin.
- **CJ covers two picks at once.** Booking.com hotels already route through CJ; DiscoverCars and Rentalcars.com (Europe) are on CJ too. One network account, one payout.
- **Disclosure rules from the hotel note apply here too:** a plain-words commission line by the links, `rel="sponsored"`, privacy.html names each network.
- **Where the link would go:** the airport block in Plate 03 ("rentals") and `transport.js`'s car estimate are the natural spots. Both read existing data; no new column is needed.
- **The 1% pay-later rate** at Rentalcars.com means many bookings pay almost nothing. DiscoverCars' profit-share model avoids that.
