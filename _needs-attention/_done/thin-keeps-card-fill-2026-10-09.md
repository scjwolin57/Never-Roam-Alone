## Things to weigh before choosing

- 173 of the 237 are in cities outside the 412-city hood-picks scope, where the pipeline had never run. 119 of those have no pick in any card.
- Empty cards are mostly a data limit, not proof the hood is not touristy: many venues have no website or only a login-walled Facebook/Instagram page, and the Google budget is nearly spent (trial ends 2026-10-30).
- Most of the other (non-thin) neighborhoods outside the 412 scope also have empty eat / coffee / drink cards today. Applying the rule only to thin keeps would remove them while leaving equally empty original hoods.
- Strict removal leaves 13 guides with 1 neighborhood, 50 with 2, 82 with 3, 48 with 4.

Options: (A) strict, remove all with any empty card; (B) remove only the ones with no pick at all in eat, coffee and drink; (C) keep all, empties stay honestly empty and the picks pipeline fills them as the budget allows.
