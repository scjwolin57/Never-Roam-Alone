# Akko (Acre) as a day trip from Tel Aviv: your call

Found 2026-09-22 in the research for the 68 cities with no day trips. Nothing changed on the site.

**Question:** should Akko's Old City (UNESCO, about 1 h 30 min north of Tel Aviv by car or train) be listed as a
full-day trip from Tel Aviv?

- The researcher left it out: the US State Department advises against travel "north of Route 85", and
  Route 85 starts at Akko itself, so it could not tell which side of the line the city is on.
- The independent checker disagreed: Akko sits at the road's western end, is open and heavily visited, and
  neither the US nor the UK advice names it.
- Our rule: no day trip in an area under a Level 4 / "advise against all travel" warning.

**Options:** leave it out (safe default, what happens if you don't answer), or list it as a full-day trip.

Tel Aviv already gets 3 confirmed trips without it: Caesarea and Zichron Ya'akov (half), Masada (full).

## RESOLVED 2026-09-23
Jeff: add it. Akko (Acre) and the Dead Sea (Ein Bokek) were added to Tel Aviv's full-day trips next to Masada, in
day-trips.js, citydata/tel-aviv.json and the sheet's Full-Day Trips cell. Rule behind it (decisions.md): one country's
travel advisory never disqualifies research.
