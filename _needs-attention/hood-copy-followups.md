## Your calls

1. **Lilongwe, "Kumbali / Lumbadzi (near airport)".** The description, landmark and only hotel are in Kumbali (east side of the city); the map pin is at the airport, 19 km away. Suggested fix: move the pin to Kumbali Cultural Village (-13.9393, 33.8411, OpenStreetMap) and rename the neighborhood "Kumbali". Yes or no?
2. **Paramaribo, "Tourtonne".** The pin sits on "Tourtonne Garden", a housing project north of town. The area people call Tourtonne runs along Tourtonnelaan, 2 to 4 km further south. No high-end or mid-range hotel is within 1.5 km of the pin, and "Tourtonne Market" is not on any map. Pick one:
   - **A.** Keep the pin. Landmark becomes "Tourtonne Garden"; hotels become budget SB7 only (high-end and mid-range left empty).
   - **B.** Move the pin to the north end of Tourtonnelaan (about 5.845, -55.149, by the Tourtonne III school). Greenheart and Q-Inn (both on Costerstraat) are then about 1.4 km away and can stay. Catch: it comes within about 0.3 km of the Flora & Rainville pin, so the two neighborhoods may overlap.
