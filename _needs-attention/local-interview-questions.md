# "Interview a Local" — Candidate Questions

Pick the 10 best. Form will be signed-in members only, reviewed before publishing.
Auto-filled: name (from account), city. Optional field: "How long have you lived here?"


1. What's something about daily life here that surprises visitors? (rhythm, customs, quirks)
2. What's a local phrase, habit, or unwritten rule a visitor should know? (tipping, greetings, queueing)
3. Describe your city's personality in three words. (great pull-quote) 
4. One neighborhood beyond the tourist center worth wandering — and why?
5. A local artist, shop, market, or event you'd tell a first-time visitor not to miss?
6. What do locals complain about — and secretly love anyway?
7. What's considered rude here that visitors do without realizing?
8. What's the one thing everyone here argues about? (best bakery, which neighborhood, a sports rivalry...)
9. What dish or drink can you only get right here — and where's it done best?
10. What's the late-night food ritual after a night out?
11. What's overrated on every "must eat" list — and what should replace it?
12. When is the worst time to visit — and when's the best time?
13. What's a free thing to do here that beats most paid attractions?
14. Which famous sight is worth the crowds, and which one can visitors skip?
15. What would make a visitor instantly win over locals?
