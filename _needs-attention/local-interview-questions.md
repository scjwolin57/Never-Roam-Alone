# "Interview a Local" — Candidate Questions

Pick the 10 best. Form will be signed-in members only, reviewed before publishing.
Auto-filled: name (from account), city. Optional field: "How long have you lived here?"

## Batch 1 (original 10)

1. What's something about daily life here that surprises visitors? (rhythm, customs, quirks)
2. What do tourists always get wrong about your city? (myths worth busting)
3. Where do you actually go on your day off? (not where you send visitors)
4. What's the best time of day (or year) to experience the city at its truest?
5. What's a local phrase, habit, or unwritten rule a visitor should know? (tipping, greetings, queueing)
6. Describe your city's personality in three words. (great pull-quote)
7. What's changing about the city that visitors should see now, before it's gone (or after it's finished)?
8. Where's your go-to meal that costs less than a fancy dinner? (dish + place)
9. One neighborhood beyond the tourist center worth wandering — and why?
10. A local artist, shop, market, or event you'd tell a first-time visitor not to miss?

## Batch 2 (more options)

### Essence & identity
11. What sound, smell, or sight instantly means "home" to you?
12. If your city were a person at a party, who would it be?
13. What's a moment or place here that still gives you goosebumps, even after years?
14. What do locals complain about — and secretly love anyway?
15. What's the biggest difference between the postcard version of your city and the real one?

### Daily life & culture
16. Walk us through a perfect ordinary Sunday for you here.
17. What time does your city really wake up, and when does it go to sleep?
18. What's a tradition or festival locals care about way more than tourists realize?
19. What's considered rude here that visitors do without realizing?
20. What's the one thing everyone here argues about? (best bakery, which neighborhood, a sports rivalry...)

### Food & drink
21. What dish or drink can you only get right here — and where's it done best?
22. Where do you take out-of-town family for their first meal?
23. What's the late-night food ritual after a night out?
24. What's overrated on every "must eat" list — and what should replace it?

### Practical insider knowledge
25. What's the smartest way to get around that visitors never use?
26. What's a money-saving trick every local knows?
27. When is the worst time to visit — and when's the underrated sweet spot?
28. What's a free thing to do here that beats most paid attractions?
29. Which famous sight is worth the crowds, and which one can visitors skip?

### Connection & people
30. Where's the easiest place to strike up a conversation with a local?
31. What would make a visitor instantly win over locals?
32. What's one thing you wish visitors did more of while they're here?
