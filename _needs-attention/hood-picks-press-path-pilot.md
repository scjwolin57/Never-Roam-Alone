# Hood picks press-path pilot: result (2026-09-25)

**Question for Jeff:** make the press path a standard step after each batch, or stop it here? Recommendation: **stop it**
(keep the 5 confirmed picks). It finds very little, and what it finds is wrong more often at the first check.

## What was tried
Candidates skipped only because no page of their own loaded (no website, login wall, dead site) in the 5 worst batch 4
cities: Tbilisi, Riyadh, Krabi, Jakarta, Johor Bahru. They could be picked when outside sources proved each point
(CHECKLIST.md section F): open within 12 months, address from OpenStreetMap or a second source, the name in two
independent sources, kind and note from a reputable dated article or guide. Round 2 added local city guides (Jeff's call).

## Why it finds so little
- Most no-page venues have no dated coverage anywhere: only Tripadvisor, Wanderlog, Instagram, TikTok or delivery apps.
- Krabi has no Michelin selection and no editorial guide covering its small venues; lovekrabitown.com lists about 30
  businesses as coupon partners. Time Out Riyadh, the likeliest source for Riyadh, blocks automated reading.
- Where press exists, it tends to cover venues that already have a website, which the normal step already picks.
