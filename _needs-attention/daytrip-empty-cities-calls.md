## Wave 3 (2026-09-22)

1. **Debre Bizen Monastery from Asmara (full).** Checked and real: an Orthodox monastery from the 1350s on a
   2,460 m peak, a steep hike up from Nefasit. **Women are not allowed in.** Held out of the list.
   Keep it (the blurb says "men-only"), or leave it out? Visitor reports also mention a permit from the church
   office in Asmara; no official source confirms that.
2. **Annandale Falls from St. George's (half).** Kept as a day trip. It is in Willis, a rural village about
   4.6 km from the Carenage (closer than the Lance aux Epines neighborhood). Day trip, or move it to landmarks?
3. **El Calafate landmarks.** Estancia Nibepo Aike is proposed as a full-day trip (56 km, inside Los Glaciares).
   But Estancia Cristina and the Upsala boat trip, also far out in the park, are listed as El Calafate
   *landmarks*. By the rule "a landmark is inside the city", those two look like day trips. Move them?
4. **Kpatawee Waterfall from Monrovia (full)** sits right at the 3 h limit (tourism board: about 3 h, last
   45 min on a dirt track, 4WD advised). The US advisory says no road travel outside Monrovia after dark, so it
   is a dawn-to-dusk trip. Kept; say if you want it out.
5. **Savai'i from Apia (full)** is tight: 2 h 20 to the ferry port at Salelologa, and the lava fields and
   blowholes in the blurb are 45-60 min further. Kept; say if you want it out.
6. **Dropped, could come back with one fact:**
   - Otse from Gaborone: travel time fine (40-45 min), but no current, dated source says whether visitors can
     enter the Manyelanong vulture reserve (sources disagree: permit in advance / sign in at the gate / a fee).
     A phone call to Botswana's wildlife department (DWNP) would settle it.
   - Carriacou from St. George's: the ferry changed its timetable on 19 Aug 2026 and the new times are only in
     images. If a 9:00-out, 17:00-back day still works, it is a full-day trip.
